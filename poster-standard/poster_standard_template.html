TREMORWATCH

AI-Powered Seismic Risk Intelligence for Disaster Management & Emergency Response
Poster Battle  •  15 Sept 2026  •  ACM Student Chapter, SVKM IOT Dhule
Rehan Shaikh  ·  Aman Ansari  ·  Kamil Mirza

Abstract

TREMORWATCH turns the live USGS global feed into emergency-response intelligence. From 11,143 events in 30 days it validates the catalog against Gutenberg–Richter (b = 1.15), maps 5°-grid risk zones where significant (M ≥ 5.0) events concentrate, and triages every newly detected event with a Random Forest model. Under strict walk-forward cross-validation (8,912 unseen future events, zero leakage), flagged events are 13.3× more likely to be significant than a random alert, and the system catches 79% of significant events — faster, better-informed response.

Process Diagram

Objectives

  • Fast triage — rank detected events by response priority in under one second
  • Risk zoning — decide where to pre-position teams, caches and drills
  • Situational awareness — one live picture of global seismic activity and severity
  • Trustworthy evaluation — leak-free validation responders can rely on

Problem Statement

Earthquakes cannot be predicted with certainty — but their where and likelihood are strongly patterned, and response is won or lost in the first minutes. When a significant event is detected, responders must answer immediately: how severe is it, which events deserve action, and where are we already prepared?

Without structured data intelligence, alert overload and guesswork delay those answers. TREMORWATCH converts a public seismic stream into them — continuously, at machine speed.

Innovation

  • An honest risk layer, not a false "prediction" — posterior risk zones plus per-event ML triage
  • Walk-forward evaluation on 8,912 unseen future events — zero leakage, the standard most posters skip
  • The model independently learned the Ring of Fire — location + depth carry ~90% of the signal
  • 13.3× lift over the 1.5% base rate, 79% catch of significant events — misses shown on purpose

Methodology

  • Data — USGS global feed (public GeoJSON, no API key); 13 Aug – 12 Sept 2026; 11,143 events
  • Validation — Gutenberg–Richter fit b = 1.15 (expected ≈ 1.0); catalog health confirmed
  • Risk zones — 5° grid; P(M ≥ 5.0); minimum 15 events per zone for stability
  • Model — Random Forest, 300 trees, balanced classes; features known at detection: grid location, depth, hour, day
  • Evaluation — 4 sequential hold-out windows; threshold 0.30; precision / recall / lift

Conclusion

Seismic response can be faster and smarter. Combining observed risk zones with leak-free ML triage, TREMORWATCH turns a public data stream into actionable priorities — resources pre-positioned where risk concentrates, responders paged only for events that matter. The same zone → triage → alert architecture generalises to cyclones, floods and any hazard with a live data feed.
