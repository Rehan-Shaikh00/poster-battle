01 The Problem

Earthquakes cannot be predicted with certainty — but their where and likelihood are strongly patterned. When a significant event strikes, every minute of the response gap costs lives and property.

  • Fast triage — which detected events deserve an immediate response?
  • Risk zones — where should teams & supplies be pre-positioned?
  • Situational awareness — what happened, where, and how severe?

05 Frequency Law
b = 1.15 ≈ 1.0 — the live data obeys the Gutenberg–Richter law, the fundamental frequency–magnitude rule of seismology.
06 Magnitude Mix
Small events dominate: only 189 of 11,143 (1.7%) are significant (M ≥ 5.0) — yet those drive the response.
